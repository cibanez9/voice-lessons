Meet your teacher

I’m a soprano and a full-time elementary music teacher in Lake County, where I teach more than 650 students and lead an after-school choir. Helping people find a voice they trust is my daily work.

I studied voice at Rollins College, am completing a master’s in music at the University of Central Florida, and hold a Florida teaching certificate in music for grades K–12. I’ve taught private voice before: I watch how each singer learns, adjust my approach, and make it comfortable to get things wrong on the way to getting them right.

What we’ll work on

  • Healthy vocal technique

    Breath, posture, and tone that let you sing freely without strain.

  • Repertoire

    Songs chosen with you, from art song and choral music to musical theatre.

  • Diction in four languages

    English, Italian, French, and German, studied through recital-level coursework.

  • Performance confidence

    Preparing for auditions, solo festivals, recitals, or simply for the joy of it.
